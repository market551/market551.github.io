About EASY.

An independent, free publication that explains how financial markets actually work.

What EASY. is

EASY. is an independent, free educational publication about financial markets. It explains how markets work from first principles: what trade and money are, why markets exist, who takes part, and how prices form. It then looks at each major market (forex, commodities, stocks, bonds, futures, options and crypto) on its own terms.

Who it is for

EASY. is for people who are new to financial markets and want the basics explained clearly. It is also for experienced traders, investors and students who want to revisit the fundamentals, check how a market is structured, or see where a common explanation oversimplifies. The aim is to simplify the concepts, not to dilute them, and to make learning the subject easier at any level.

Our approach

Logic first, evidence second, labels last. We explain the mechanism before the jargon, and we separate fact, data, interpretation and hypothesis. Where a claim needs a source we mark it as such rather than guessing.

What EASY. is not

EASY. is not a service. It does not offer trading signals, courses, subscriptions, paid content, account management, personal advice or financial products, and it does not promote brokers. It does not tell anyone what to buy or sell. It is a free publication that explains how markets work.

Educational content only

Everything on this site is general education. It is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice, and it does not take your personal situation into account. Trading and investing involve risk, including the loss of money. Speak to a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

Corrections

If you find a factual error or an unsupported claim, please tell us through the contact page. We correct mistakes and aim to cite sources for important figures.

Advertising

The site is planned to be supported by advertising. Ads, when present, are clearly labelled and kept separate from the educational content. See the privacy policy for details.
