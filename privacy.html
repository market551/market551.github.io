Privacy Policy

What this site collects, and how future advertising is handled.

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

EASY. is a static website. It has no user accounts, no comment system and no sign-up forms, and we do not ask you for personal information.

Information we collect

We do not run our own analytics or store visitor data. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs, according to its own privacy statement.

Cookies and advertising

The site does not currently set cookies of its own and does not currently load advertising. We plan to display ads from third-party advertising networks in the future. Those networks may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, and may personalise them. When ads are enabled, this page will be updated to name the providers, explain what they collect, and describe how to opt out, and we will show a consent notice where the law requires one.

External links

Pages may link to external websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data. Since we do not collect it directly, requests about data held by advertising or hosting providers should go to those providers. You can also contact us through the contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date above shows the latest revision.

Educational disclaimer

Content on EASY. is for education only and is not financial advice. See the about page.
