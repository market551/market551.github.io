Before Forex, stocks, futures or crypto, there was a much simpler problem: people had things others wanted.

⚡ FAST

Trade is simply
an exchange.

YOU HAVE

GOODS

Something useful to another person.

THEY HAVE

OTHER GOODS

Something useful to you.

RESULT

EXCHANGE

Both sides agree to trade.

THE PROBLEM

But direct exchange
has a problem.

Imagine you have wheat and want shoes. The person with shoes must also want your wheat at the same time.

01

YOU WANT SHOES

You have wheat to exchange.

02

THE SHOEMAKER WANTS SOMETHING

But perhaps they don't want wheat.

03

THE EXCHANGE FAILS

The problem isn't desire. It's coordination.

02 · MONEY

better ways to exchange.

Money became a commonly accepted medium that could be exchanged for goods and services.

01

Something people accept in exchange.

02

MEASUREMENT

A common way to express economic value.

03

Value can move between people and across time.
