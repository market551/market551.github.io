03 · ORIGIN

BEFORE THE MODERN FOREX MARKET

Currency exchange did not begin with computers or trading platforms. It developed from the basic need to exchange different forms of money and settle transactions between people, businesses and economies.

01

TRADE

People exchanged goods and different forms of money across regions.

02

CURRENCY

States and economies developed their own monetary systems.

03

EXCHANGE

Specialized markets and financial institutions developed to facilitate currency conversion.

04

GLOBAL MARKET

Technology eventually connected participants across financial centers around the world.

04 · MECHANICS

HOW DOES FOREX WORK?

Forex prices are expressed as currency pairs because buying one currency means simultaneously selling another.

01

EUR/USD

The first currency is the base currency. The second is the quote currency.

→
02

PRICE

→
03

TRANSACTION

05 · PARTICIPANTS

WHO IS IN THE MARKET?

Forex is not one single group of traders. Different participants enter the market for different reasons.

01

BANKS

Provide liquidity, facilitate transactions and manage currency exposure.

02

BUSINESSES

Exchange currencies when operating or trading internationally.

03

INVESTORS

Take currency exposure as part of investment, hedging or speculation.

04

CENTRAL BANKS

Manage monetary policy and hold foreign-exchange reserves.

05

GOVERNMENTS

Interact with currencies through economic policy, trade and public finance.

06

RETAIL TRADERS

Access currency-price exposure through financial intermediaries and trading platforms.

06 · PRICE

HOW IS THE PRICE FORMED?

07 · DRIVERS

WHAT MOVES A CURRENCY?

Currency prices can respond to changes in economic conditions, expectations, policy and the flow of capital.

01

INTEREST RATES

Changes in monetary policy can alter the relative attractiveness of assets denominated in a currency.

+
02

ECONOMIC DATA

Growth, inflation, employment and other economic information can change expectations.

+
03

CAPITAL FLOWS

Money moving between countries and assets can create demand for particular currencies.

+
04

EXPECTATIONS

Markets react not only to what happens, but also to what participants expect to happen next.

+
08 · HISTORY

THE MODERN FOREX SYSTEM

The foreign-exchange system changed substantially as the international monetary system evolved.

PAST

FIXED SYSTEMS

International monetary arrangements historically used different mechanisms for determining currency values.

CHANGE

FLOATING RATES

Major currencies increasingly moved toward exchange rates determined by market forces.

TECH

ELECTRONIC MARKETS

Electronic communication and computing transformed how prices and transactions moved between financial institutions.

TODAY

GLOBAL NETWORK

Modern currency markets connect financial institutions, businesses, investors and intermediaries across regions.
