The 8-Layer Pedagogical Architecture

Every major topic, market, and article published on EASY is systematically constructed across eight sequential layers of depth:

STAGE 01
FAST

A plain-language definition readable in under 15 seconds. An intelligent beginner immediately grasps the fundamental concept without stumbling over technical gatekeeping.

STAGE 02
VISUAL

Clear diagrams, SVG flows, and order-matching schematics. Visualizing the movement of assets, liabilities, and matching queues establishes instant spatial comprehension.

STAGE 03
LOGIC

The first-principles economic imperative: why must this mechanism exist? We trace the friction, human incentive, and coordination breakdown that requires this tool.

STAGE 04
HISTORY

The specific historical bottleneck or crisis that forced this mechanism into existence. Understanding why Amsterdam created shares or Chicago created futures removes the mystery.

STAGE 05
EVIDENCE

Strict demarcation between verified Fact, statistical Data, institutional Interpretation, and theoretical Hypothesis. Where verified empirical evidence is missing, we write "SOURCE REQUIRED".

STAGE 06
CONTEXT

How this specific asset class interacts with the broader macroeconomy: central bank balance sheets, interest rate curves, physical supply logistics, and regulatory clearing.

STAGE 07
TEST

An interactive self-assessment widget that tests your understanding of the underlying causal logic, providing instant verification and conceptual rationale.

STAGE 08
DEEP

Institutional edge cases, microstructure bottlenecks, non-linear pricing models, settlement cycles, and boundary conditions for advanced researchers.
